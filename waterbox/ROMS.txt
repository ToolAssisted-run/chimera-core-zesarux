The ROM built into this core
============================

48.rom (16384 bytes, SHA-1 5ea7c2b824672e914525d1d5c419d71b84a426a2)
is the ZX Spectrum 48K's ROM, as ZEsarUX itself is distributed with it
(src/48.rom at the pinned commit), byte for byte the 48.rom Fuse is
distributed with. The ZX Spectrum Next's fast start runs on it.

It is copyright Amstrad, who allow distribution of the ROMs but retain
the copyright. You may not sell the ROMs or embed the ROMs in hardware,
although it is allowed to sell a product which contains the ROMs, so long
as the charge is being made for the product, not for the ROMs themselves.
(These are the terms as Fuse's roms/README.copyright states them; see
<http://groups.google.com/group/comp.sys.amstrad.8bit/msg/c092cc4d4943131e>.)

Not in this package: the Next's boot ROM (tbblue_loader.rom), which the
user supplies, and any SD card image.
